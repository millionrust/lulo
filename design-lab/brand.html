Lulo OS brand mark

OverviewMenu barIcons Brand

Six candidates for the mark that replaces the placeholder R. Each is shown in the top bar (dark and light, at the bar's 14 pt glyph size), alone at 16 / 18 / 22 px, in full colour at 128 px, as a 1024 app tile on the rmac icon grid (scripts/build-icons.py) and in the Lulo OS lockup. Judged on legibility at 16 px first, then memorability, then story.

Chosen: A. Segment — the only candidate that is a solid silhouette at 14 px (so it carries the same visual weight as the Mac's own menu glyph and our status icons), is instantly readable as one round object, and still tells the fruit story when it is large.

A. Segment Chosen

The lulo cut open: an orange fruit whose four green seed chambers radiate from the centre. A solid disc with four teardrop windows — at 14 pt it has the weight of the Mac's own menu glyph, reads as one round object in dark and light bars, and becomes the fruit when large. Holes are ≥ 3 px at 1×, septa ≥ 1.3 units, rind 1.4 units (the status icons' stroke).
