# Lulo OS brand

The Lulo OS mark is called **Segment**. It is a round fruit cut open, with four
seed chambers radiating from the centre. The candidates it was chosen from, and
the reasons, are in [design-lab/brand.html](../design-lab/brand.html).

A lulo (naranjilla) is a bright orange fruit from the Andes. When you cut it
open you see green, translucent flesh in four segments around the centre, and
the mark is that cross-section drawn as one shape.

## The mark

The mark is a disc with four teardrop windows. It is drawn on a 14 × 14 grid
from circles and their tangent lines, so it stays crisp at small sizes:

| Part | Size (grid units) |
|---|---|
| Disc | radius 7 |
| Chamber (×4, pointing up, right, down and left) | inner circle radius 0.62, 1.25 from the centre; outer circle radius 1.9, 3.72 from the centre; joined by tangent lines |
| Rind (disc edge to chamber) | 1.38, about the same as the status icons' strokes |
| Wall between chambers | 0.53 where the chambers meet at the centre, widening to 1.46 near the rind |

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `shell/assets/status/rmac.svg` | Top-bar template glyph (the leftmost menu), tinted by the shell |
| `assets/brand/lulo-mark-template.svg` | The same glyph in black, for other one-colour uses |
| `assets/brand/lulo-mark.svg` | Full-colour mark, 128 px |
| `assets/icons/lulo*.svg` | App-icon tile in default, dark, clear and tinted, generated by `scripts/build-icons.py` |
| `assets/brand/lulo-os-lockup.svg`, `…-dark.svg` | Mark and wordmark, for light and dark backgrounds |
| `crates/system-settings/assets/icons/lulo.svg` | White glyph for the orange Lulo OS tile in System Settings › General › About |

In the top bar the glyph is 14 × 14 pt (`LOGO_GLYPH`), centred in the 34 pt
logo slot. That's the height of the Mac's own menu glyph and 1 pt taller than
the 13 pt status icons. As a template glyph it takes the bar's text colour, so
it's white on a dark bar and black on a light one.

## Wordmark and lockup

The wordmark is "Lulo OS" in **Inter Display SemiBold**, converted to outlines.
Tracking is −1.2 % and the word space is 86 % of Inter's default. Kerning comes
from Inter's own tables. Inter ships with the shell and is licensed under SIL
OFL 1.1.

In the lockup:

- The mark is 1.55 × the cap height.
- The mark is centred on the cap height.
- The gap between the mark and the "L" is 0.4 × the mark's diameter.

## Clear space and minimum sizes

- **Clear space:** keep half the mark's diameter clear on every side of the
  mark. Around the lockup, keep the mark's radius clear.
- **Template glyph:** 14 px at 1×. Below that, the chamber windows close up.
- **Colour mark:** 16 px. The green chambers need contrast against the orange.
- **Lockup:** the mark must be at least 20 px tall.
- **Seeds:** the version with pale seed dots in each chamber (in the design
  lab) is for 64 px and larger only.

## Don'ts

- Don't draw it as a whole fruit in profile, or add a leaf, stem or bite. The
  mark must never read as an apple.
- Don't add rainbow or multicolour stripes, or colour the chambers anything
  other than Pulp Green.
- Don't rotate the mark. The chambers point up, down, left and right.
- Don't outline the mark, add a drop shadow to it (the app tile has its own
  lighting), or put it on a busy image without a plate.
- Don't re-typeset the wordmark in another font or weight, or change its
  spacing.
- Don't use the colour mark where a template glyph belongs: the top bar and
  other monochrome UI take the one-colour version.
