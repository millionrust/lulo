1 · The desktop at rest

Everything rmac shows when nothing is open: wallpaper, a transparent menu bar, and the Dock. Compare with target/evidence/rmac-current.png — today rmac paints an opaque black bar 40 px tall and flat glyph icons. Those two things alone account for most of the missing Mac feeling.

2 · An open menu

Measured: panel 269 px wide, item pitch 24 px. macOS 27 removed icons from ordinary menu rows. Menus open instantly — no fade in; on selection the row blinks once, then the menu fades out over 150 ms.

Values marked S until the owner photographs the real lock screen — it cannot be screenshotted from inside a session. Structure is certain: blurred wallpaper, date above a very large clock at the top, avatar and password field at the bottom.
