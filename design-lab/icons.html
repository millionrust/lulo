Dock icons — rmac beside the Mac

Every icon is generated by scripts/build-icons.py from one glass recipe: a superellipse plate (n = 4.2, 824 of 1024), a vertical body gradient, a soft top-left sheen, a 1 px rim brightest at the top-left and bottom-right corners, and a frosted glyph with its own gradient, bright edge and soft shadow. The Trash, folders and documents have no plate. Regenerate with python3 scripts/build-icons.py; --check fails on stale files.

Appearances

The Dock uses the default appearance in both light and dark mode, as the Mac does. Dark, clear and tinted variants are emitted to assets/icons for surfaces that ask for them.
